My Life

Chef. Developer. Painter. Poet. — five crafts, one kitchen.
The Full Picture

I am a chef, a programmer, and a painter — all at once.

Twenty years in professional kitchens taught me discipline, timing, and how to lead a team under fire. But alongside the knives and the flames, I've always built things with my hands and my mind — sketches, code, and now real software systems.

Today I bring that rare mix into every kitchen I work in: culinary craft sharpened by a developer's logic, and seasoned with an artist's eye.

The Poet Inside

I write poetry — in Arabic and in English.

Words are my other seasoning. When the kitchen goes quiet and the last plate has been sent out, I write. Poetry about family, about home, about the small moments that feed a person more than any dish ever could.

I believe a chef who writes poetry cooks differently. He plates with intention. He reads the table. He understands that every guest brings a story — and every dish is my reply.

"I cook with fire,
I code with silence,
and I write with what's left of the heart."
— Chef Ahmed Hammad
Why It Matters

Every craft makes the kitchen work more unique.

Programming taught me to think in systems — mise en place is just good architecture. Painting taught me colour, balance, and how a plate should feel before it's even tasted. Poetry taught me empathy and rhythm — the tempo of a service, the beat of a busy pass.

Put all of that together and you get a chef who doesn't just cook — he designs experiences, builds the systems behind them, and tells the story on the plate.
