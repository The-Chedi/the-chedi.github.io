Lifestyle

Behind the apron — stories, philosophy, and everyday inspiration.

My Philosophy

Cooking is storytelling on a plate.

Twenty years in professional kitchens taught me that a great dish is more than technique — it is memory, culture, and generosity. From the spice markets of Alexandria to the polished dining rooms of Dubai, I have learned to cook with respect for ingredients, for my team, and for every guest.

Every plate should tell a story: where it came from, who made it, and why it matters. That's the standard I bring to The Chedi El Gouna, every service.

Daily Ritual

Mise en place, every morning.

Before the first guest arrives, the kitchen is already alive. Prep lists, stock rotation, station briefing — every detail matters. I believe in leading from the front: hands-on, calm, and precise.

Outside the kitchen, I follow food trends, travel for flavors, and spend time with family — the best reminder of why hospitality exists.

"Doin' it fresh — every single plate."

— Chef Ahmed Hammad
