Beetroot & Goat Cheese Salad

The Chedi · September 7, 2026
  1. Wash and clean the red and yellow baby beetroot thoroughly.
  2. Cook the baby beetroot until tender, cool and peel carefully.
  3. Cook the larger beetroot until tender, cool, peel and cut into small wedges.
  4. Peel the carrot and cut into fine julienne or thin slices.
  5. Peel and finely grate the fresh ginger.
  6. Prepare the dressing: mix red grape vinegar, ginger and cooking cream until smooth.
  7. Toast the whole hazelnuts until golden and aromatic, cool and roughly chop.
  8. Wash and dry the watercress, green leaves and Lollo Rosso lettuce thoroughly.
  9. Arrange beetroot, lettuce, green leaves, watercress and orange pieces on plate, add goat cheese.
  10. Drizzle with the dressing, finish with crystal sugar and toasted hazelnuts. Serve chilled.

Ingredients

  • Baby Beetroot (Red)30 g
  • Baby Beetroot (Yellow)30 g
  • Goat Cheese50 g
  • Watercress10 g
  • Orange20 g
  • Hazelnut Whole12 g
  • Sugar Crystal30 g
  • Green Leaves Fresh5 g
  • Lettuce Lollo Rosso (Green)5 g
  • Lettuce Lollo Rosso (Red)5 g
  • Beetroot50 g
  • Ginger Fresh10 g
  • Red Grape Vinegar25 ml
  • Rich Cream Juhayna50 ml
Prep10 min
Serves1 guest
