THE CHEDI
El Gouna Red Sea
Creamy Miso Sauce
Use For : Lamb Chops Dish
prep time : 10 minutes
Batch : I
New Menu
DATE:September 8, 2026
INVOICE #120
INGREDIENTQTYUNITNOTE
White Miso Paste50gram
Heavy Cream200ml
Chicken Stock100ml
Butter30gram
Garlic, minced8gram
Soy Sauce10ml
Sugar5gram
  • Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  • Add garlic and saute gently without browning.
  • Add stock and bring to a light simmer.
  • Whisk in the miso paste until completely dissolved.
  • Add cream, soy sauce and sugar.
  • Simmer gently until slightly thickened and glossy.
  • Finish and adjust seasoning.
  • Strain for a smooth sauce if needed.
