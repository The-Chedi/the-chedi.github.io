Tabbouleh Salad

Ingredients

  • Parsley500 g
  • Tomato Big200 g
  • Mint Green100 g
  • Spring Onion50 g
  • Onion Red20 g
  • Lemon Fresh30 g
  • Olive Oil15 ml
  • Pomegranate Molasses5 ml
  • Salt Table5 g
  • Black Pepper Powder2 g
  • Green Ice Cream Burrata0.03 g

Method

  1. Wash and sanitize the parsley, mint, tomatoes and spring onions thoroughly.
  2. Drain and dry the herbs and vegetables well to prevent excess moisture.
  3. Finely chop the parsley and place in a mixing bowl.
  4. Finely chop the fresh mint and add to the parsley.
  5. Dice the tomatoes into small even pieces and add to the bowl.
  6. Finely slice the spring onion and red onion, mix with herbs and tomatoes.
  7. Prepare the dressing: combine fresh lemon juice, olive oil and pomegranate molasses.
  8. Season the dressing with salt and black pepper, mix well.
  9. Add the dressing to the salad and gently toss until evenly coated.
  10. Plate the tabbouleh neatly, finish with green ice cream burrata, serve chilled.
