Veal Tonnato

Ingredients

  • Veal Fillet150 g
  • Tonnato Sauce50 g
  • Mixed Lettuce50 g
  • Capers10 g
  • Pastrami Powder3 g

⚠ Allergen information

🍽️ Starter 🥗 Salad 🌶️ Mild
⏱️ Prep: 8 min 🍽️ Serves: 1 guests

✦ Garnish

✦ Microgreens ✦ Pastrami Powder

Method

  1. Arrange the veal slices neatly on a chilled flat plate, slightly overlapping.
  2. Spread the tonnato sauce lightly over the veal, keeping the meat partially visible.
  3. Arrange the mixed lettuce neatly to one side of the plate for height and freshness.
  4. Sprinkle the capers evenly over the veal and sauce.
  5. Finish with a light dusting of pastrami powder.
  6. Clean the plate edges and serve chilled.
